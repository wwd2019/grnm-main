# Publication and review scope

The result tables and notebooks are designed to make the existing analysis inspectable, not to claim that the targeted GLI3 outcome is an unbiased independent benchmark. HES4 was explicitly added to the candidate universe after inspection; HES5 and PAX6 were prioritized in earlier analysis. The final pando+GRN model is trained with other TFs' Pando edges and shares GRNBoost2 data with the comparator. Its score is an uncalibrated binary ranking score, and the model does not assign an activation/repression sign. The three top-10 candidates are HES5, PAX6 and HES4, but two already occur as GLI3 edges in first-stage Pando. The original manuscript must disclose these points and the use of motif-unfiltered GRNBoost2 results in the displayed comparison.

AUC/AP evaluation uses Pando-inferred positives and unlisted pairs as background; they are not independent experimentally verified ground truth. SCENIC's PAX6(+) and HES5(+) regulons describe those TFs' predicted targets, not proof of GLI3→PAX6/HES5 regulation. Default cisTarget produced no GLI3 regulon. Group AUCell patterns may reflect differing cell-type compositions. See the original full_archive manuscript audit in the sibling directory for pending biological source, accession, ethics and attribution checks.

No distribution license, publication rights, journal choice, or data release approval has been supplied. The package includes human RNA data and must be checked by the authors for permission, de-identification and appropriate public access before external distribution. This package does not itself publish or send anything.

The planarian example uses a different species, model and candidate set from the human extension. Its Hes1-associated top-10 table is a selected-regulator demonstration generated from saved classifier weights, not a matched cross-species or baseline benchmark. The seven-class scores are not calibrated probabilities or independent proof of regulatory direction.
